# Health Tracking and Visualization Knowledge Base

## Health Metrics and Biomarkers

### Vital Signs and Basic Measurements
**Blood Pressure:**
- **Normal ranges**: Systolic <120 mmHg, Diastolic <80 mmHg
- **Tracking frequency**: Daily for hypertensive patients, weekly for monitoring
- **Factors affecting readings**: Time of day, caffeine, stress, arm position, cuff size
- **Visualization**: Line charts showing trends over time, color-coded zones (normal, elevated, high)

**Heart Rate:**
- **Resting heart rate**: 60-100 bpm (lower for athletes)
- **Target heart rate zones**: 50-85% of maximum heart rate (220 - age)
- **Heart rate variability (HRV)**: Indicator of autonomic nervous system health
- **Tracking methods**: Wearable devices, manual pulse checks, ECG monitors

**Body Weight and Composition:**
- **BMI calculation**: Weight (kg) / Height (m)²
- **Healthy BMI range**: 18.5-24.9
- **Body fat percentage**: Men 10-20%, Women 16-25% (varies by age)
- **Muscle mass tracking**: Important for aging populations and athletes
- **Visualization**: Weight trend lines, body composition pie charts, progress photos

**Sleep Metrics:**
- **Total sleep time**: 7-9 hours for adults
- **Sleep efficiency**: Time asleep / time in bed × 100%
- **Sleep stages**: Light, deep, REM sleep percentages
- **Sleep onset latency**: Time to fall asleep (<30 minutes normal)
- **Wake episodes**: Number and duration of nighttime awakenings

### Laboratory Values and Biomarkers
**Metabolic Health:**
- **Fasting glucose**: 70-99 mg/dL normal, 100-125 mg/dL prediabetic, ≥126 mg/dL diabetic
- **HbA1c**: <5.7% normal, 5.7-6.4% prediabetic, ≥6.5% diabetic
- **Insulin levels**: Fasting 2-25 mIU/L
- **Lipid panel**: Total cholesterol <200 mg/dL, LDL <100 mg/dL, HDL >40/50 mg/dL

**Inflammatory Markers:**
- **C-reactive protein (CRP)**: <3.0 mg/L low risk, 3.0-10.0 mg/L moderate risk
- **Erythrocyte sedimentation rate (ESR)**: Age-dependent normal ranges
- **Interleukin-6 (IL-6)**: Marker of systemic inflammation

**Hormonal Health:**
- **Thyroid function**: TSH 0.4-4.0 mIU/L, Free T4 0.8-1.8 ng/dL
- **Cortisol**: Morning 6-23 mcg/dL, varies throughout day
- **Testosterone**: Men 300-1000 ng/dL, Women 15-70 ng/dL
- **Vitamin D**: 30-100 ng/mL optimal range

### Fitness and Performance Metrics
**Cardiovascular Fitness:**
- **VO2 max**: Maximum oxygen uptake during exercise
- **Resting heart rate trends**: Lower indicates improved fitness
- **Recovery heart rate**: How quickly heart rate returns to baseline
- **Exercise capacity**: Duration and intensity of sustained activity

**Strength and Power:**
- **One-rep maximum (1RM)**: Maximum weight lifted for single repetition
- **Power output**: Force × velocity measurements
- **Muscular endurance**: Repetitions at submaximal loads
- **Flexibility and mobility**: Range of motion measurements

**Activity Tracking:**
- **Daily steps**: 8,000-10,000 steps recommended
- **Active minutes**: Moderate and vigorous activity time
- **Calorie expenditure**: Total daily energy expenditure
- **Exercise frequency**: Number of workout sessions per week

## Data Collection Methods and Technologies

### Wearable Devices and Sensors
**Fitness Trackers:**
- **Accelerometers**: Measure movement and activity levels
- **Heart rate sensors**: Optical (photoplethysmography) and electrical (ECG)
- **GPS tracking**: Location, distance, pace for outdoor activities
- **Environmental sensors**: Temperature, humidity, UV exposure

**Smartwatches:**
- **Advanced health monitoring**: ECG, blood oxygen, fall detection
- **Workout tracking**: Automatic exercise recognition and metrics
- **Sleep monitoring**: Movement and heart rate-based sleep staging
- **Stress monitoring**: Heart rate variability analysis

**Medical Devices:**
- **Blood pressure monitors**: Automatic cuff-based measurements
- **Glucose monitors**: Continuous glucose monitoring (CGM) systems
- **Smart scales**: Body weight, fat percentage, muscle mass, bone density
- **Pulse oximeters**: Blood oxygen saturation monitoring

### Mobile Health Applications
**Symptom Tracking Apps:**
- **Pain scales**: Visual analog scales, numeric rating scales
- **Mood tracking**: Daily mood ratings, emotional state logging
- **Medication adherence**: Pill reminders and compliance tracking
- **Symptom journals**: Free-text and structured symptom reporting

**Nutrition Tracking:**
- **Calorie counting**: Food database integration and barcode scanning
- **Macronutrient tracking**: Protein, carbohydrate, fat intake
- **Micronutrient analysis**: Vitamin and mineral intake assessment
- **Meal timing**: Intermittent fasting and meal schedule tracking

**Exercise and Activity Apps:**
- **Workout logging**: Exercise type, duration, intensity, sets/reps
- **Running/cycling apps**: GPS tracking, pace, elevation, route mapping
- **Strength training**: Progressive overload tracking and program management

### Manual Tracking Methods
**Paper-Based Logs:**
- **Food diaries**: Detailed meal and snack recording
- **Exercise journals**: Workout plans and performance tracking
- **Symptom diaries**: Daily health status and medication effects
- **Sleep logs**: Bedtime, wake time, sleep quality ratings

**Digital Spreadsheets:**
- **Custom tracking templates**: Personalized health metrics

## Data Visualization Techniques

### Chart Types and Applications
**Line Charts:**
- **Best for**: Trends over time (weight, blood pressure, glucose levels)
- **Features**: Multiple data series, trend lines, confidence intervals
- **Annotations**: Medication changes, lifestyle interventions, events
- **Time scales**: Daily, weekly, monthly, yearly views

**Bar Charts:**
- **Best for**: Comparing categories (weekly exercise minutes, daily steps)
- **Variations**: Grouped bars for multiple metrics, stacked bars for components
- **Color coding**: Performance zones, goal achievement status
- **Interactive features**: Drill-down capabilities, filtering options

**Scatter Plots:**
- **Best for**: Relationships between variables (sleep vs. mood, exercise vs. weight)
- **Correlation analysis**: Trend lines and correlation coefficients
- **Clustering**: Identifying patterns and outliers
- **Multi-dimensional**: Bubble charts with size representing third variable

**Heat Maps:**
- **Best for**: Patterns across time periods (sleep quality by day of week)
- **Color scales**: Intensity mapping for metric values
- **Calendar views**: Daily health metrics in monthly grid format
- **Activity patterns**: Exercise frequency, medication adherence

**Pie Charts and Donut Charts:**
- **Best for**: Composition data (macronutrient breakdown, sleep stages)
- **Limitations**: Avoid for more than 5-7 categories
- **Interactive features**: Hover details, segment highlighting
- **Progress indicators**: Goal completion visualization

### Dashboard Design Principles
**Information Hierarchy:**
- **Primary metrics**: Most important health indicators prominently displayed
- **Secondary information**: Supporting details and context
- **Tertiary data**: Detailed breakdowns accessible through interaction
- **Alert systems**: Highlighting concerning trends or values

**Visual Design:**
- **Color psychology**: Red for alerts, green for goals met, blue for information
- **Consistency**: Standardized color schemes and chart types
- **Accessibility**: Color-blind friendly palettes, sufficient contrast
- **White space**: Avoiding clutter and information overload

**User Experience:**
- **Customization**: User-defined metrics and layout preferences
- **Responsive design**: Optimal viewing on mobile, tablet, desktop
- **Loading performance**: Fast data retrieval and rendering
- **Offline capabilities**: Local data storage and sync when connected

### Advanced Visualization Techniques
**Predictive Analytics:**
- **Trend forecasting**: Projecting future health metrics based on current data
- **Risk modeling**: Probability calculations for health outcomes
- **Goal projection**: Timeline estimates for achieving health targets
- **Confidence intervals**: Uncertainty ranges in predictions

**Comparative Analysis:**
- **Peer comparisons**: Anonymous benchmarking against similar demographics
- **Historical comparisons**: Current vs. previous time periods
- **Goal tracking**: Progress toward personal health objectives
- **Intervention analysis**: Before/after comparison of lifestyle changes

**Multi-variate Analysis:**
- **Correlation matrices**: Relationships between multiple health metrics
- **Principal component analysis**: Identifying key health factors
- **Cluster analysis**: Grouping similar health patterns
- **Time series decomposition**: Seasonal and trend components

## Health Data Standards and Interoperability

### Data Formats and Standards
**HL7 FHIR (Fast Healthcare Interoperability Resources):**
- **Purpose**: Standard for exchanging healthcare information electronically
- **Resources**: Patient, Observation, DiagnosticReport, Medication
- **Benefits**: Interoperability between different health systems
- **Implementation**: RESTful APIs for data exchange

**SNOMED CT (Systematized Nomenclature of Medicine Clinical Terms):**
- **Purpose**: Comprehensive clinical terminology system
- **Coverage**: Diseases, symptoms, procedures, medications
- **Benefits**: Standardized coding for health data
- **Integration**: Mapping local terms to SNOMED codes

**LOINC (Logical Observation Identifiers Names and Codes):**
- **Purpose**: Universal standard for laboratory and clinical observations
- **Coverage**: Lab tests, vital signs, clinical measurements
- **Benefits**: Consistent identification of health measurements
- **Usage**: Mapping device data to standardized codes

### Data Quality and Validation
**Data Accuracy:**
- **Sensor calibration**: Regular validation against gold standard measurements
- **Outlier detection**: Identifying and flagging unrealistic values
- **Missing data handling**: Interpolation methods and gap identification
- **User input validation**: Range checks and consistency rules

**Data Completeness:**
- **Tracking compliance**: Percentage of expected data points collected
- **Reminder systems**: Notifications for missing measurements
- **Data imputation**: Statistical methods for filling gaps
- **Quality scores**: Overall data reliability metrics

**Data Consistency:**
- **Cross-validation**: Comparing measurements from multiple sources
- **Temporal consistency**: Checking for logical progression over time
- **Unit standardization**: Converting between different measurement units
- **Reference ranges**: Age and gender-specific normal values

### Privacy and Security Considerations
**Data Encryption:**
- **At rest**: Encrypted storage of health data
- **In transit**: Secure transmission protocols (HTTPS, TLS)
- **Key management**: Secure encryption key storage and rotation
- **Access controls**: Role-based permissions and authentication

**HIPAA Compliance:**
- **Protected health information (PHI)**: Identifying and securing sensitive data
- **Minimum necessary rule**: Limiting data access to required information
- **Audit trails**: Logging all data access and modifications
- **Business associate agreements**: Third-party vendor compliance

**User Consent and Control:**
- **Informed consent**: Clear explanation of data collection and use
- **Granular permissions**: User control over specific data types
- **Data portability**: Ability to export personal health data
- **Right to deletion**: User ability to remove personal data
