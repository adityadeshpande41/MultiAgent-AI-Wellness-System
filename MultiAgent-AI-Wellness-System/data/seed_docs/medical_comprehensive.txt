# Comprehensive Medical and Health Knowledge Base

## Preventive Health and Wellness

### Primary Prevention
**Cardiovascular Health:**
- Regular aerobic exercise: 150 minutes moderate or 75 minutes vigorous weekly
- Blood pressure monitoring: <120/80 mmHg optimal
- Cholesterol management: Total <200 mg/dL, LDL <100 mg/dL, HDL >40 mg/dL (men), >50 mg/dL (women)
- Smoking cessation: Reduces heart disease risk by 50% within one year
- Limit alcohol: 1 drink/day (women), 2 drinks/day (men)

**Cancer Prevention:**
- Sun protection: SPF 30+, avoid peak hours (10am-4pm)
- Tobacco avoidance: Leading preventable cause of cancer
- Healthy weight maintenance: BMI 18.5-24.9
- Limit processed meats and excessive red meat consumption
- Regular screenings: Mammograms, colonoscopies, Pap smears as recommended

**Diabetes Prevention:**
- Maintain healthy weight: 5-10% weight loss reduces risk by 58%
- Regular physical activity: Improves insulin sensitivity
- Balanced diet: Emphasize whole grains, lean proteins, healthy fats
- Monitor blood glucose: Fasting <100 mg/dL, HbA1c <5.7%

### Screening Guidelines (General Population)
**Blood Pressure:**
- Age 18-39: Every 3-5 years if normal
- Age 40+: Annually
- High risk: More frequently as recommended

**Cholesterol:**
- Men 35+, Women 45+: Every 5 years
- Earlier if risk factors present

**Diabetes:**
- Age 45+: Every 3 years
- Earlier if overweight with risk factors

**Cancer Screenings:**
- Mammography: Women 50-74, every 2 years
- Cervical cancer: Women 21-65, Pap smear every 3 years
- Colorectal: Age 50-75, colonoscopy every 10 years or other methods

## Mental Health and Psychological Wellness

### Anxiety Disorders
**Generalized Anxiety Disorder (GAD):**
- Symptoms: Excessive worry, restlessness, fatigue, difficulty concentrating
- Duration: Symptoms present for 6+ months
- Management: Cognitive-behavioral therapy, relaxation techniques, medication if severe

**Panic Disorder:**
- Symptoms: Sudden intense fear, heart palpitations, sweating, trembling
- Duration: Peak intensity within minutes
- Management: Breathing exercises, grounding techniques, professional therapy

**Social Anxiety:**
- Symptoms: Fear of social situations, avoidance behaviors, physical symptoms
- Management: Gradual exposure therapy, social skills training, mindfulness

### Depression
**Major Depressive Disorder:**
- Symptoms: Persistent sadness, loss of interest, fatigue, sleep disturbances, appetite changes
- Duration: Symptoms present for 2+ weeks
- Risk factors: Family history, chronic illness, major life changes, substance abuse

**Seasonal Affective Disorder (SAD):**
- Symptoms: Depression during specific seasons (usually winter)
- Management: Light therapy, vitamin D supplementation, regular exercise

### Stress Management Techniques
**Acute Stress Response:**
- Deep breathing: 4-7-8 technique (inhale 4, hold 7, exhale 8)
- Progressive muscle relaxation: Tense and release muscle groups
- Grounding: 5-4-3-2-1 technique (5 things you see, 4 you hear, etc.)

**Chronic Stress Management:**
- Regular exercise: Reduces cortisol levels, improves mood
- Mindfulness meditation: 10-20 minutes daily
- Time management: Prioritization, delegation, boundary setting
- Social support: Maintain relationships, seek help when needed

### Sleep Disorders
**Insomnia:**
- Types: Difficulty falling asleep, staying asleep, or early awakening
- Causes: Stress, anxiety, poor sleep hygiene, medical conditions
- Management: Sleep hygiene, cognitive-behavioral therapy for insomnia (CBT-I)

**Sleep Apnea:**
- Symptoms: Loud snoring, gasping during sleep, daytime fatigue
- Risk factors: Obesity, neck circumference >17" (men), >16" (women)
- Management: Weight loss, CPAP therapy, positional therapy

**Sleep Hygiene Principles:**
- Consistent sleep schedule: Same bedtime and wake time daily
- Sleep environment: Cool (65-68°F), dark, quiet
- Pre-sleep routine: Relaxing activities 1 hour before bed
- Avoid: Caffeine 6 hours before bed, large meals 3 hours before bed, screens 1 hour before bed

## Common Health Conditions

### Respiratory Health
**Common Cold:**
- Symptoms: Runny nose, sneezing, mild cough, low-grade fever
- Duration: 7-10 days
- Management: Rest, fluids, humidifier, saline nasal rinse
- When to seek care: High fever, severe headache, difficulty breathing

**Influenza:**
- Symptoms: High fever, body aches, fatigue, dry cough
- Duration: 5-7 days
- Prevention: Annual flu vaccine, hand hygiene
- Management: Antiviral medications if started within 48 hours

**Asthma:**
- Triggers: Allergens, exercise, cold air, stress, infections
- Symptoms: Wheezing, shortness of breath, chest tightness, cough
- Management: Avoid triggers, rescue inhalers, controller medications
- Emergency signs: Severe difficulty breathing, inability to speak in full sentences

### Gastrointestinal Health
**Gastroesophageal Reflux Disease (GERD):**
- Symptoms: Heartburn, regurgitation, difficulty swallowing
- Triggers: Spicy foods, citrus, chocolate, caffeine, alcohol, large meals
- Management: Dietary modifications, weight loss, elevate head of bed, medications

**Irritable Bowel Syndrome (IBS):**
- Symptoms: Abdominal pain, bloating, diarrhea or constipation
- Triggers: Stress, certain foods (FODMAPs), hormonal changes
- Management: Dietary modifications, stress reduction, probiotics, medications

**Food Poisoning:**
- Symptoms: Nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, abdominal cramps, fever
- Duration: 1-7 days depending on pathogen
- Management: Hydration, electrolyte replacement, gradual return to normal diet
- When to seek care: Severe dehydration, high fever, blood in stool

### Musculoskeletal Health
**Lower Back Pain:**
- Causes: Muscle strain, herniated disc, poor posture, sedentary lifestyle
- Acute management: Ice first 24-48 hours, then heat, gentle movement
- Prevention: Core strengthening, proper lifting technique, ergonomic workspace
- Red flags: Numbness/tingling in legs, loss of bladder/bowel control

**Arthritis:**
- **Osteoarthritis**: Wear-and-tear of joint cartilage, common in weight-bearing joints
- **Rheumatoid Arthritis**: Autoimmune condition affecting joint lining
- Management: Exercise, weight management, anti-inflammatory medications, physical therapy

**Tendinitis:**
- Common sites: Shoulder (rotator cuff), elbow (tennis/golfer's elbow), Achilles
- Causes: Overuse, repetitive motions, sudden increase in activity
- Management: Rest, ice, compression, elevation (RICE), gradual return to activity

### Cardiovascular Health
**Hypertension (High Blood Pressure):**
- Categories: Normal <120/80, Elevated 120-129/<80, Stage 1 130-139/80-89, Stage 2 ≥140/90
- Risk factors: Age, family history, obesity, high sodium intake, sedentary lifestyle
- Management: Lifestyle modifications, DASH diet, regular exercise, medications if needed

**Hyperlipidemia (High Cholesterol):**
- Risk factors: Diet high in saturated/trans fats, genetics, obesity, diabetes
- Management: Heart-healthy diet, regular exercise, weight management, statins if indicated
- Target levels: LDL <100 mg/dL (or <70 mg/dL for high-risk patients)

### Endocrine Health
**Type 2 Diabetes:**
- Risk factors: Obesity, sedentary lifestyle, family history, age >45
- Symptoms: Increased thirst/urination, fatigue, blurred vision, slow-healing wounds
- Management: Blood glucose monitoring, dietary modifications, exercise, medications
- Complications: Cardiovascular disease, neuropathy, nephropathy, retinopathy

**Thyroid Disorders:**
- **Hypothyroidism**: Fatigue, weight gain, cold intolerance, dry skin, constipation
- **Hyperthyroidism**: Weight loss, rapid heartbeat, heat intolerance, anxiety, tremor
- Management: Hormone replacement therapy (hypothyroid), antithyroid medications (hyperthyroid)

## Women's Health

### Menstrual Health
**Normal Menstrual Cycle:**
- Length: 21-35 days (average 28 days)
- Duration: 3-7 days
- Flow: 5-40 mL total volume

**Common Issues:**
- **Dysmenorrhea**: Painful periods, managed with NSAIDs, heat, exercise
- **Irregular periods**: May indicate hormonal imbalances, stress, weight changes
- **Heavy bleeding**: >80 mL or >7 days, may require medical evaluation

### Reproductive Health
**Contraception Options:**
- Barrier methods: Condoms, diaphragms, cervical caps
- Hormonal methods: Birth control pills, patches, rings, injections, implants
- Long-acting reversible contraception (LARC): IUDs, implants
- Permanent methods: Tubal ligation, vasectomy

**Pregnancy Health:**
- Prenatal vitamins: Folic acid 400-800 mcg daily
- Weight gain: 25-35 lbs for normal BMI
- Exercise: Generally safe to continue pre-pregnancy activities
- Avoid: Alcohol, smoking, high-mercury fish, raw/undercooked foods

### Menopause
**Symptoms:**
- Hot flashes, night sweats, mood changes, sleep disturbances
- Vaginal dryness, decreased libido, bone density loss

**Management:**
- Lifestyle: Regular exercise, healthy diet, stress management
- Hormone replacement therapy: Benefits and risks should be discussed with healthcare provider
- Non-hormonal options: SSRIs, gabapentin, clonidine for hot flashes

## Men's Health

### Prostate Health
**Benign Prostatic Hyperplasia (BPH):**
- Symptoms: Difficulty starting urination, weak stream, frequent urination
- Risk factors: Age, family history, obesity
- Management: Lifestyle modifications, medications, surgical options for severe cases

**Prostate Cancer Screening:**
- PSA testing: Discuss with healthcare provider starting age 50 (45 for high-risk)
- Digital rectal exam: Part of routine screening
- Risk factors: Age, race (higher in African Americans), family history

### Testosterone Health
**Low Testosterone (Hypogonadism):**
- Symptoms: Fatigue, decreased libido, erectile dysfunction, mood changes, muscle loss
- Causes: Aging, obesity, chronic illness, medications
- Management: Lifestyle modifications, testosterone replacement therapy if indicated

### Cardiovascular Risk
**Men vs. Women:**
- Men develop heart disease 7-10 years earlier than women
- Higher risk of sudden cardiac death
- Important to address risk factors early: smoking, hypertension, diabetes, high cholesterol

## Emergency Situations and When to Seek Care

### Medical Emergencies (Call 911)
**Heart Attack Signs:**
- Chest pain/pressure, pain radiating to arm/jaw/back
- Shortness of breath, nausea, sweating, lightheadedness
- Women may have atypical symptoms: fatigue, back pain, nausea

**Stroke Signs (FAST):**
- Face drooping, Arm weakness, Speech difficulty, Time to call 911
- Additional signs: Sudden severe headache, vision loss, confusion

**Severe Allergic Reaction (Anaphylaxis):**
- Difficulty breathing, swelling of face/throat, rapid pulse
- Widespread rash, severe nausea/vomiting, dizziness
- Use epinephrine auto-injector if available, call 911

### Urgent Care Situations
**Seek Same-Day Care:**
- High fever (>103°F or 39.4°C)
- Severe abdominal pain
- Difficulty breathing (not severe)
- Suspected fracture or severe sprain
- Deep cuts requiring stitches
- Severe headache with neck stiffness

### Primary Care Follow-up
**Schedule Appointment:**
- Persistent symptoms lasting >1-2 weeks
- New or changing moles
- Unexplained weight loss or gain
- Changes in bowel or bladder habits
- Persistent fatigue or mood changes

## Health Maintenance by Age Group

### Young Adults (18-39)
**Focus Areas:**
- Establish healthy lifestyle habits
- Mental health awareness and stress management
- Reproductive health and family planning
- Injury prevention (sports, motor vehicle, occupational)
- Substance abuse prevention

### Middle Age (40-64)
**Focus Areas:**
- Chronic disease prevention and management
- Cancer screening initiation
- Cardiovascular risk assessment
- Bone health (especially women approaching menopause)
- Work-life balance and stress management

### Older Adults (65+)
**Focus Areas:**
- Fall prevention and balance training
- Cognitive health and dementia prevention
- Medication management and polypharmacy awareness
- Social engagement and mental health
- End-of-life planning and advance directives
