# Advanced Fitness Knowledge Base

## Exercise Physiology

### Energy Systems
- **Phosphocreatine System**: Provides immediate energy for 0-10 seconds of high-intensity exercise
- **Glycolytic System**: Fuels moderate to high-intensity exercise for 10 seconds to 2 minutes
- **Oxidative System**: Supports low to moderate-intensity exercise for extended periods (2+ minutes)

### Muscle Fiber Types
- **Type I (Slow-twitch)**: High endurance, fatigue-resistant, better for aerobic activities
- **Type IIa (Fast-twitch oxidative)**: Moderate power and endurance, adaptable to training
- **Type IIx (Fast-twitch glycolytic)**: High power, low endurance, best for explosive movements

### Training Principles
- **Progressive Overload**: Gradually increase training stimulus (weight, reps, sets, frequency)
- **Specificity**: Training adaptations are specific to the type of exercise performed
- **Reversibility**: "Use it or lose it" - fitness gains are lost without continued training
- **Individual Differences**: People respond differently to the same training program

## Strength Training Programs

### Beginner (0-6 months)
**Full Body Routine (3x/week)**
- Squats: 3 sets x 8-12 reps
- Push-ups or Bench Press: 3 sets x 8-12 reps
- Bent-over Rows: 3 sets x 8-12 reps
- Overhead Press: 3 sets x 8-12 reps
- Planks: 3 sets x 30-60 seconds
- Deadlifts: 2 sets x 5-8 reps

### Intermediate (6-18 months)
**Upper/Lower Split (4x/week)**
**Upper Day:**
- Bench Press: 4 sets x 6-8 reps
- Bent-over Rows: 4 sets x 6-8 reps
- Overhead Press: 3 sets x 8-10 reps
- Pull-ups/Lat Pulldowns: 3 sets x 8-12 reps
- Dips: 3 sets x 10-15 reps
- Bicep Curls: 3 sets x 12-15 reps

**Lower Day:**
- Squats: 4 sets x 6-8 reps
- Romanian Deadlifts: 3 sets x 8-10 reps
- Bulgarian Split Squats: 3 sets x 10-12 each leg
- Hip Thrusts: 3 sets x 12-15 reps
- Calf Raises: 4 sets x 15-20 reps

### Advanced (18+ months)
**Push/Pull/Legs Split (6x/week)**
**Push Day:**
- Bench Press: 5 sets x 3-5 reps
- Incline Dumbbell Press: 4 sets x 6-8 reps
- Overhead Press: 4 sets x 6-8 reps
- Lateral Raises: 4 sets x 12-15 reps
- Tricep Dips: 3 sets x 10-12 reps
- Close-grip Bench Press: 3 sets x 8-10 reps

## Cardiovascular Training

### HIIT Protocols
**Tabata (4 minutes total)**
- 20 seconds all-out effort
- 10 seconds rest
- Repeat 8 rounds

**30/30 Intervals (20 minutes)**
- 30 seconds high intensity (85-90% max heart rate)
- 30 seconds recovery (60-70% max heart rate)
- Repeat 20 rounds

### LISS Guidelines
- **Zone 2 Training**: 60-70% max heart rate for 30-60 minutes
- **Benefits**: Improved fat oxidation, aerobic base, recovery enhancement
- **Frequency**: 2-4 sessions per week

## Mobility and Flexibility

### Dynamic Warm-up (Pre-workout)
1. Arm circles: 10 forward, 10 backward
2. Leg swings: 10 front-to-back, 10 side-to-side each leg
3. Hip circles: 10 each direction
4. Walking lunges with twist: 10 each side
5. High knees: 20 steps
6. Butt kicks: 20 steps

### Static Stretching (Post-workout)
1. Hamstring stretch: 30 seconds each leg
2. Quad stretch: 30 seconds each leg
3. Calf stretch: 30 seconds each leg
4. Chest doorway stretch: 30 seconds
5. Shoulder cross-body stretch: 30 seconds each arm
6. Hip flexor stretch: 30 seconds each side

## Recovery and Regeneration

### Sleep Optimization
- **Duration**: 7-9 hours for optimal recovery
- **Quality**: Deep sleep stages crucial for growth hormone release
- **Consistency**: Same bedtime and wake time daily
- **Environment**: Cool (65-68°F), dark, quiet room

### Active Recovery Methods
- **Light cardio**: 20-30 minutes at 50-60% max heart rate
- **Yoga or stretching**: 15-30 minutes daily
- **Foam rolling**: 10-15 minutes targeting tight areas
- **Walking**: 8,000-10,000 steps on rest days

### Nutrition for Recovery
- **Post-workout window**: Consume protein and carbs within 2 hours
- **Protein timing**: 20-40g protein every 3-4 hours
- **Hydration**: Replace 150% of fluid lost during exercise
- **Anti-inflammatory foods**: Berries, fatty fish, leafy greens

## Common Injuries and Prevention

### Lower Back Pain
**Prevention:**
- Strengthen core muscles (planks, dead bugs, bird dogs)
- Improve hip mobility (hip flexor stretches, pigeon pose)
- Proper lifting technique (neutral spine, engage core)

**Management:**
- Avoid bed rest; maintain gentle movement
- Apply heat for muscle tension, ice for acute inflammation
- Gentle stretching and walking

### Knee Pain
**Prevention:**
- Strengthen glutes and quadriceps
- Improve ankle mobility
- Proper footwear for activities
- Gradual progression in training intensity

### Shoulder Impingement
**Prevention:**
- Strengthen rotator cuff muscles
- Improve thoracic spine mobility
- Avoid overhead activities if painful
- Focus on posterior deltoid and rhomboid strengthening

## Periodization Strategies

### Linear Periodization
- **Weeks 1-4**: High volume, low intensity (12-15 reps)
- **Weeks 5-8**: Moderate volume, moderate intensity (8-10 reps)
- **Weeks 9-12**: Low volume, high intensity (3-5 reps)

### Undulating Periodization
- **Monday**: Heavy (3-5 reps, 85-95% 1RM)
- **Wednesday**: Moderate (6-8 reps, 75-85% 1RM)
- **Friday**: Light (12-15 reps, 60-75% 1RM)

### Block Periodization
- **Accumulation Block**: High volume, technique focus
- **Intensification Block**: Reduced volume, increased intensity
- **Realization Block**: Peak performance, competition prep

## Special Populations

### Older Adults (65+)
- **Focus**: Balance, functional movements, fall prevention
- **Frequency**: 2-3 strength sessions, daily balance work
- **Modifications**: Seated exercises, resistance bands, slower progressions

### Pregnant Women
- **Avoid**: Supine positions after first trimester, contact sports
- **Recommended**: Walking, swimming, prenatal yoga, pelvic floor exercises
- **Intensity**: Moderate (able to hold conversation during exercise)

### Youth Athletes (Under 18)
- **Emphasis**: Movement quality over quantity
- **Resistance**: Bodyweight and light weights initially
- **Recovery**: Extra attention to sleep and nutrition during growth spurts
